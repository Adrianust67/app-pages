Privacy Policy

PunchCard: TimeClock · Effective November 17, 2026

Short version: PunchCard does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you record stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

PunchCard stores the clock-in and clock-out times and optional notes that you enter. This information is saved only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any server.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No location, contacts, photos or other personal information
  • No third-party services or SDKs

Sharing you choose to do

When you print, email or share a report or timesheet, the app hands it to iOS (AirPrint, Mail or the Share sheet) only at that moment and only to the destination you choose. The developer does not receive a copy.

Backups and deletion

Your entries are part of your normal iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or your computer), handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can delete individual entries in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all its data from your device.

Children

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
