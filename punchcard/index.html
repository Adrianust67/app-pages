PunchCard: TimeClock

A simple personal time clock for iPhone.

Clock in and out with one tap, review your hours per day, week, month or year, and keep a weekly timesheet with every clock-in and clock-out time. Fix missed punches, then print or email your timesheet to compare it with your employer's records.

  • One-tap Clock In / Clock Out with a live timer
  • Reports for this week, last week, this month, last month, this year, or any date range
  • Weekly timesheet with totals per punch, per day and per week
  • Edit, add or delete punches
  • Print, email or export as CSV
  • Private: your data never leaves your iPhone
